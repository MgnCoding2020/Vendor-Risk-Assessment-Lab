# Vendor Intake — Cloud Camera Service

Captured 2026-11-02 for a vendor in use since the shop opened (2026-01-20). The two cameras were installed by the vendor and never assessed; the Connected Devices Procedure (PR-011) found the gap.

---

| | |
|---|---|
| **Vendor** | Cloud camera service (unnamed; fictional) |
| **Vendor ID** | V-010 |
| **Service** | Two indoor cameras (CAM-01 front door and customer area; CAM-02 back door and stock room) with cloud recording, a mobile app and a web viewer |
| **Internal business owner** | Olivia Carter (Owner) |
| **Intake date** | 2026-11-02 |
| **In use since** | 2026-01-20 |

---

## Data Handled by the Vendor

| Data | Classification |
|---|---|
| Video of customers, staff and delivery drivers (DATA-005) | Confidential |
| Account details for the camera app | Internal |

**No cardholder data**, provided no camera can see the keypad, PIN entry or the register screen ([PR-011](https://github.com/MgnCoding2020/grc-paper-project-coffee-shop/blob/main/05-procedures/PR-011-connected-devices.md) §5).

---

## System Access

The cameras sit on the store network (today the staff Wi-Fi, with the POS) and connect out to the vendor's cloud. The vendor can push firmware and, with permission, view footage for support.

---

## Business Criticality

Low for trading. Higher for evidence: footage supports incident and insurance claims.

---

## Regulatory & Compliance Considerations

  - Recording people: notice to customers and staff (signage), limited access, limited retention
  - Video must never capture card PIN entry
